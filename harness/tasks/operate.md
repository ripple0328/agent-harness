# Task: operate

Use for release observation, incident investigation, maintenance or retirement.

Load `harness/workflows/production-delivery.md`, the debugger role when diagnosing,
release/security rules, and `harness/templates/release-observation.md`.
Inspect source evidence, establish impact and current state, identify one cause
at a time, and test recovery through the authorized scope. Update regression
coverage and action owners; capture minimal observations and verified lessons.

Output: observed outcome, causal evidence or uncertainty, recovery/maintenance
result, owner and next action, context updates and feedback candidate.

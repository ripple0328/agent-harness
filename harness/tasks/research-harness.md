# Task: research-harness

## Use When

External research, release notes, social curation, or a scheduled sweep may
suggest an improvement to the agent harness.

## Load

- `docs/ai-fsd/README.md`
- `docs/ai-fsd/sources.md`
- `docs/ai-fsd/application-ledger.md`
- `harness/workflows/continuous-research-improvement.md`
- `harness/workflows/signal-triggered-improvement.md`
- `harness/roles/harness-maintainer.md`
- `harness/templates/research-signal.md`
- relevant source artifacts and project evidence

## Steps

Follow `harness/workflows/continuous-research-improvement.md`.

## Output

- dated research note
- source-registry updates
- at most one bounded experiment or an explicit no-change decision
- verification result
- application-ledger update
- cross-project adoption or feedback recommendation when applicable

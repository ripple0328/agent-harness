## Decision Meanings

- `applied`: verified and incorporated into the canonical harness.
- `experiment`: in a bounded trial; not yet a durable default.
- `deferred`: plausible, but missing evidence, a suitable project, or timing.
- `rejected`: reviewed and does not meet relevance, evidence, or cost threshold.
- `reverted`: previously applied but removed after negative evidence.
